Frequently asked questions

Who can use SmartReatile?
Only verified students with a valid college email can create an account, so every listing you see is from someone on your own campus.
Is it free to list an item?
Yes — publishing a listing on SmartReatile is completely free, with no hidden fees when your item sells.
How do payments and meetups work?
Buyers and sellers arrange payment and a safe, on-campus meeting spot directly through in-app chat — we recommend well-lit, public locations like the library or student center.
Can I report a suspicious listing?
Every product page has a Report option, and our moderation team reviews flagged listings within 24 hours.
